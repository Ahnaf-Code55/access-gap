# Public-source example and attribution

The built-in public-source example uses the Natural History Museum's official [Accessibility at South Kensington page](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/visit/access-at-south-kensington.html), reviewed on **28 October 2026**. The page states it was last updated in March 2026 but gives no exact observation date for each facility.

The example contains three short attributed paraphrases: a steep ramp at the Central Entrance, lift access with a maintenance caveat, and the presence of accessible toilets on the museum map. It copies no photos, maps, logos, or lengthy passages. The museum is not affiliated with this project.

The review date is not treated as an onsite verification date. Evidence records intentionally leave `observedAt` empty, and the engine keeps them unknown pending confirmation. Missing doorway dimensions and connecting-route evidence remain missing. The example does not combine different museum entrances into a false contradiction.

Fictional contradictions are only demonstrated in the separately labelled Riverside Station example. Neither example is a route planner or safety assessment. No private trip, personal profile, or disability data is included.
